MDOE407 · Online Education Model Design

Managing Underperformance

Simulation prototypes for a course on performance conversations for new managers. Set at Lumera Aesthetics, a fictional company. All people and data are invented.

Four levels, each giving less support than the one before it: a guided walkthrough, part-task practice, judgement in the room, then the conversation with no scaffolding at all.
